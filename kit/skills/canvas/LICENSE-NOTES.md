# Licence notes — what is inside this kit

## The kit itself
The skill (SKILL.md, reference/, components/, scripts/, template/, examples/, the design tokens, and the sounds and grounds it
draws in code) is shared free. Use it to make your own videos, personal or commercial. Please don't resell it or repackage it
as a paid product.

## Sounds, music, footage
- **Sound effects** are generated by `scripts/synth-sfx.py` from noise, sine waves and envelopes. No samples, no third-party
  audio. Free to use in your videos.
- **No music** is included. Use a track you have the rights to (`scripts/bed-mix.py`), or none.
- **No footage** of anyone is included. The style was measured off a public reel; only the measurements are here.

## Your responsibility
Everything a video uses (a painting, a photo, a page, a clip, a music track) needs your rights to it. Use public-domain
paintings from Wikimedia Commons (the skill writes each one's licence into PLAN.md) and your own photos and screens.
