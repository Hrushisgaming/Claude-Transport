## Pacing and workflow per style
- **aesthetic-lofi**: long shots (4-8 s), slow cuts on the bar line, no effects. Workflow: montage.py --every 4 > analyze --style aesthetic-lofi > final > export.
- **anime-amv**: cut every beat in the drop; slow-mo hold on the key frame before it. Workflow: montage.py --every 1 > analyze --style anime-amv --music > preview > final > export.
- **comedy-meme**: fast; zoom the beat BEFORE the joke, hold the punchline, then cut. Workflow: silence_cut > transcribe > analyze --style comedy-meme > audioforge sfx > final > export.
- **cyber-tech**: glitch on every state change; one calm beat before the reveal. Workflow: analyze --style cyber-tech > devices > audioforge score > preview > final.
- **documentary**: a picture per 0.6-1.5 s; sound on every change. Workflow: transcribe > source footage > plan with sources > plancheck > final > verify.py.
- **horror-chase**: slow dread first; counters (5 LEFT ... LAST ONE) at each down; silence 0.5 s before the last hit. Workflow: analyze --style > hud_events for downs > plan > preview > final > export.
- **hype-highlight**: hook in 1.5 s; a hit every 1.5-3 s, every 0.6-1.2 s in the finale; one freeze on the best moment. Workflow: analyze --style > hud_events (optional) > plan > preview > final > export.
- **music-video**: cut every 2 beats (1 in the drop, 4 in the intro); strongest clip on the downbeat of the drop. Workflow: montage.py --music > analyze --style music-video --music > preview > final > export.
- **news-explainer**: steady; one visual change every 3-5 s; numbers on screen when spoken. Workflow: silence_cut > transcribe > overlays > final.
- **product-ad**: a new picture every 1-2 s, cut on the beat, one number or claim per card. Workflow: montage.py > analyze --style product-ad > audioforge score > preview > final > export.
- **real-estate**: 4-6 s per shot, cut on the bar line, a soft whoosh between rooms. Workflow: montage.py --every 4 > analyze --style real-estate > final.
- **sport**: slow-mo on the key play, then a hit on the action frame. Workflow: analyze --style sport > follow reframe > plan > preview > final > export.
- **talking-head**: remove silences > 0.6 s; a slow push-in every 20-30 s is enough. Workflow: silence_cut > transcribe > analyze --style talking-head > captions > audioforge score (soft bed) > final > export.
- **trailer**: few, big hits: every card gets a riser; the biggest hit is followed by a clean 1 s of silence then the title. Workflow: analyze --style > plan with title cards > audioforge score > preview > final > export.
- **travel**: 3-5 s per shot, cut on the beat of a mid-tempo bed; one slow-mo on the best view. Workflow: montage.py with music > analyze --style travel > plan > preview > final > export.
- **tutorial**: cut dead air, zoom (punch) only to point at the thing being clicked. Workflow: silence_cut > transcribe > analyze --style tutorial > step counters > final > export.
- **vlog**: cut every sentence; a punch-in on emphasis; remove pauses (silence_cut.py). Workflow: silence_cut > transcribe > analyze --style vlog > captions > audioforge score (quiet bed) > final > export.
- **wedding-memory**: 3-6 s per shot, dissolves; keep the speech in the vows, music under everything else. Workflow: montage.py --every 4 > analyze --style wedding-memory > final.
