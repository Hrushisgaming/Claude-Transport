# What's inside this kit

## The kit itself
The skill, its scripts and its generated sounds are shared free by NextPhase. Use them to make your own videos, personal or commercial. Please don't resell it or repackage it as a paid product.

## Music
`music/song.m4a` is included so the edit has music straight away. **Its licence hasn't been confirmed.** A platform could flag it, and that's more of a risk on a monetized video or an ad. If that matters to you, give Claude a track you have the rights to.

## Sound effects
Everything in `sfx/` is generated by `scripts/synth_sfx.py` from noise and sine waves, shaped to lengths and levels measured from the reference reel. No samples and no third-party audio. Free to use in your videos.

## The reference
The style was studied from a public reel, credited at the top of `reference/study.md`. Only measurements and written descriptions are here. No footage, frames, music or sounds from that reel are in this kit, and this kit isn't made with or endorsed by its creator.

## Worked examples
The example code comes from one real video, so it holds that video's timings and wording. None of its media is included.

## Your responsibility
You need the rights to anything you add to a video: music, a company's film, a news photo, a clip from someone else's video. Editor V2's archive search keeps a credits list with each file's licence. The final call is yours. Higgsfield has its own terms, and credits you spend there are between you and Higgsfield.
